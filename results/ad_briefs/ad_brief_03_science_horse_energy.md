### 🎥 Core Clip (3–47 sec)
**Visual:** Teacher demonstrating with hands; cut to student leaning in with curiosity

> **Teacher:** "Now look at this horse. The horse can decide WHEN to run, and WHERE to go — all on its own. That is what we call self-propelled energy. The energy comes from INSIDE.
>
> On the other hand — look at the tractor. The tractor also has energy. But where does it get it from?"
>
> **Student:** "The engine."
>
> **Teacher:** "Exactly! From the engine. Someone has to start it. Someone has to give it fuel. That energy is FORCED from outside.
>
> So living things? Self-propelled. Non-living things? Forced energy. Got it?"
>
> **Student:** "Yeah."
